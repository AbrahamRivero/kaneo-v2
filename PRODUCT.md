# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Teams and organizations seeking self-hosted project management. Developers, small teams, privacy-conscious businesses who want control over their data. Users value performance, simplicity, and deployment flexibility over feature density.

## Product Purpose

Kaneo is a self-hosted project management platform built on the principle that less is more. Unlike bloated enterprise tools, every feature exists because it solves a real problem, not because it looks impressive in a demo. The goal is a tool that gets out of the way and lets teams focus on building great products.

Success looks like: teams having a fast, focused project management experience that respects their workflow, not forcing them to adapt to the tool's limitations.

## Brand Personality

Clean, minimal, performant. The brand voice is confident but understated - no flashy marketing copy, just solid functionality. Words like "invisible," "fast," "own your data."

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards with cookie-cutter blue accents and generic icons
- Complex enterprise tools (Asana, Jira) with overwhelming feature density
- Linear/Figma clones that blindly follow popular SaaS design patterns
- Extreme minimalism that feels bare, unfinished, or unpolished

## Design Principles

1. **Invisible design** - The best tools are invisible. Kaneo should amplify natural workflow, not demand attention.
2. **Essential only** - Every feature earns its place. If it doesn't solve a real problem, it doesn't belong.
3. **Performance first** - Fast interactions, minimal bundle, no unnecessary loading states. Speed is a feature.
4. **Deployment flexibility** - Designed for self-hosting from day one. Works in Docker, Kubernetes, anywhere.
5. **Purposeful clarity** - Clear visual hierarchy, intentional whitespace, no decorative excess.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance
- WCAG 2.2 AA compliance (focus indicators, drag-drop alternatives)
- Screen reader optimized with enhanced ARIA support
- Keyboard navigation throughout
